2×2 determinant

abcd  det = a·d − b·c

Geometry

The columns are two vectors. |det| is the area of the parallelogram they span (volume in 3×3). A negative sign means the picture is mirrored.

3×3 cofactor expansion

det A = a11C11 + a12C12 + a13C13
Cij = (−1)i+j·Mij, where Mij is the 2×2 determinant left after deleting row i and column j.

Sign pattern (−1)i+j

Sarrus’ rule

Add the three main-diagonal products, subtract the three anti-diagonal products.
